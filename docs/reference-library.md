## Collection workflow once access works

1. Browse the official qualification pages and inspect the applicable specification/version and route.
2. For each public candidate, record subject, board, specification, document type, edition/year, examination session/component where relevant, source URL, access conditions and verification date.
3. Verify paper/mark-scheme/report matches using their printed identifiers. Do not infer identity from filenames alone.
4. Respect terms and licensing. Publicly viewable material is not automatically licensed for downloading, rehosting or model processing. Catalogue links where copying is not authorised; do not bypass locked resources.
5. Import only authorised materials through the library, retaining source and page references. Clearly separate official assessments from original practice.
6. Supplement with reputable teaching resources only after checking accuracy, applicability and permissions. YouTube and poem websites mentioned in the other conversation were not supplied and are not verified sources here.
